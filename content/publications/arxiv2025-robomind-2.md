While data-driven imitation learning has revolutionized robotic manipulation, current approaches remain constrained by the scarcity of large-scale, diverse real-world demonstrations. We present RoboMIND 2.0, a comprehensive real-world dataset comprising over 310K dual-arm manipulation trajectories collected across six distinct robot embodiments and 739 complex tasks. To support contact-rich and spatially extended tasks, the dataset incorporates 12K tactile-enhanced episodes and 20K mobile manipulation trajectories. Complementing this physical data, we construct high-fidelity digital twins of the real-world environments and release an additional 20K-trajectory simulated dataset for robust sim-to-real transfer. To exploit RoboMIND 2.0, we propose MIND-2, a hierarchical dual-system framework optimized through offline reinforcement learning, combining a high-level semantic planner with a low-level Vision-Language-Action executor.
